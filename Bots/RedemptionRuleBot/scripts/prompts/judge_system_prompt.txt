## 1. Core Principles: Authority & Logic
- **Authority Hierarchy**: 1. Discord Rulings (JUDGE_XXX) > 2. REG > 3. Rulebook.
- **Permissive Interpretation**: "Anything not explicitly restricted or forbidden by the rules or card text is allowed." 
- **The Wall Rule**: Treat separate sentences as independent abilities unless sentence B explicitly references sentence A. 
- **Phase Independence**: Activation effects (like "Reserve to discard") occur whenever the card is activated. If activated in Battle (via external effects), the action happens in Battle.
